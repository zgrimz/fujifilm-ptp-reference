# Sources

This record started from other people's reverse engineering, and much of it is still theirs.

When original observations and captures can be inspected, prefer them. A `[Published: X]` tag identifies the source actually inspected for a claim, which might report its own observation, relay another project's work, or describe an implementation. Credit the original observer where known and identify indirect citations and unclear measurement provenance alongside the claim. A relay isn't independent corroboration of its source. The following source notes separate reported measurements and body scope from relayed material and identify what this reference uses.

## Reverse-engineering projects

### [eggricesoy/FilmKit](https://github.com/eggricesoy/filmkit)

License: MIT.

Reported measurements: From an X100VI, the bank property identities, the write order with Kelvin after the white balance mode, and most value encodings, cross-referenced against X RAW Studio captures and preset scans. The X100VI firmware versions 1.31–1.32 appear in connection reports in its issues; they don't independently establish the firmware of the original measurement sweep. Its issues hold other owners' data: X RAW Studio captures and a FilmKit log from an X-T50 fw 1.31 (#12) and an X-T30 fw 2.01 (#13), an X-T3 fw 5.11 `GetDeviceInfo` and preset-save capture (#5), X100V captures and a 4-property dataset whose storage-mode interpretation is inferred (#1, #8), an X-M5 fw 1.30 log (#18), the `0xD183` trigger values on an X-T50 (#15), and an X100VI exposure-compensation test (#6).

Relayed material: Its film simulation table comes from rawji, with 17–20 from tkcranny/fxraw; its upload and session code is ported from rawji. FilmKit itself triggers every conversion with `0`, the reduced render.

Used here: The bank property identities, the Kelvin-after-white-balance order, the encodings tagged `[Published: FilmKit]` (menu-confirmed on an X-T50 where the tag says so), and the public captures and logs in its issues, decoded in [captures](captures/README.md). Its profile-table errors, grain UI values, and `0xD1A3` name are in [errata](docs/errata.md#filmkit).

### [macnow/filmkit](https://github.com/macnow/filmkit) (a FilmKit fork)

License: MIT.

Reported measurements: No separate measurements cited here.

Relayed material: FilmKit's code, with Exposure Bias moved to cell 5 (April 14, 2026), confirmed by an X100VI user in FilmKit #6.

Used here: Its published correction of the one-cell offset between FilmKit's and libfuji's conversion-profile indices, and the identification of cell 4 as Image Quality. The cited commit establishes this contribution, not absolute first-publication priority.

### [pinpox/rawji](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji)

License: GPL-3.0-or-later, declared in [`pyproject.toml`](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/blob/02cb559bb1555b3f77e4b246cad06c44cff56c8e/pyproject.toml); no standalone license file.

Reported measurements: From the X-T30, X100F, X-T3, and X-E5 (its tested list), the X-T30's 605-byte profile and its acceptance of a 29-cell write, grain values in the profile on an X-E5, Color Chrome FX Blue from an X RAW Studio capture, and `0x2002` for a RAF from another body, without specifying the failed operation. grawji separately places a foreign-RAF failure at the profile read after upload.

Relayed material: libfuji's `fp` field names and encodings, and libgphoto2's constants. grawji's author (`p5k369`) contributed rawji's [free-Kelvin and −2…+3 EV statements](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/b9cdca4), [foreign-RAF note](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/6fb3a73), [timeout/power-cycle advice](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/8540e98), [X-E5 grain finding](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/8d08c29), [thumbnail support](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/ccd20a7), and [TIFF/HEIF handling](https://github.com/pinpox/rawji/commit/c90ac9c). grawji [depends on the inspected rawji revision](https://github.com/p5k369/grawji/blob/7cd98fe/pyproject.toml#L12-L21). These citations share authorship and implementation lineage; their appearance in both projects is not independent corroboration.

Used here: The conversion-profile field map, the file-type cell and its output formats, the Exposure Bias encoding and rounding, the reported −2…+3 EV range, the 31 Kelvin presets of older bodies and free Kelvin on X-Processor 5, grain in the profile, the thumbnail preview, and the X100F as a converting X-Processor Pro body. Its DR 1/2/3 cell-6 writes and its toning-range claims are in errata.

### [petabyt/libfuji](https://github.com/petabyt/libfuji)

License: MIT.

Reported measurements: Nine raw `0xD185` profiles (`fp/d185/`), which are camera reads and X RAW Studio writes from an X-H1 and an X-Pro3, and from an unconfirmed 23-cell body whose file names say X-T5 but whose IOPCode `FF129504` matches X RAW Studio's X100F saved profiles in `fp/fp2`–`fp3`. The bytes are consistent with X100F; original provenance remains open. Those saved-profile XML files also carry X RAW Studio's field names and the IOPCodes of an X-H1 (`FF129506`), an X100F (`FF129504`), an X-T3 (`FF159501`), and an X-T5 (`FA179502`).

Relayed material: The Wi-Fi protocol and libgphoto2's property names, out of scope here.

Used here: The profiles, which settled the layout; the name and 1/2 encoding of cell 21 (Lens Modulation Optimizer); the two Monochromatic Color cells and their −180…+180 range; its D-Range Priority (0–3) and HDR enumerations; the `0xD212` event list; `0xD16E`; `0x901D`; and settings backup and restore. Its color-space table and its Color Chrome FX Blue indexing are in errata.

### [libgphoto2](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2) `camlibs/ptp2`

License: LGPL. The top-level [`COPYING`](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2/blob/92171a382f185f617e95cb9acecf2d43688b19ab/COPYING) is v2.1; the cited `ptp2` source headers, such as [`ptp.c`](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2/blob/92171a382f185f617e95cb9acecf2d43688b19ab/camlibs/ptp2/ptp.c), say version 2 or any later version.

Reported measurements: Its X-T5 additions of August 2026 are `0xD030`, `0xD032`, `0xD189`, and `0xD02E` as D-Range Priority by a differential probe. [PR #1302](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2/pull/1302) also supplies X-T5 NR and DR observations; the grain and color-space tables predate that change and don't establish X-T5 measurements. The three contributed camera dumps used here, under `camlibs/ptp2/cameras/`, are an X100VI fw 1.00 (its **USB RAW CONV.** mode attribution is inferred; the dump and originating report do not establish the mode), a GFX100 II in tether mode, and a GFX100S fw 1.10 whose USB mode isn't established here. They are paraphrased here and never copied (the X100VI dump carries a serial number).

Relayed material: No relayed material identified here.

Used here: Names for the vendor property codes, including the live shooting properties and `0xD184`, `0xD186`, `0xD187`, `0xD189`, `0xD20E`, and `0xD212`, and the `0xD212` event-list structure. It defines no Fujifilm property at `0xD18C` or `0xD18D` and doesn't implement the bank or conversion protocol; it names `0xD029` "Shadowing" while its own X-T5 test saw Color Chrome Effect.

### [ILFforever/fujifilm-ptp-recipes](https://github.com/ILFforever/fujifilm-ptp-recipes)

License: CC BY 4.0 (docs), MIT (examples).

Measurements and static analysis: An X-H2 (fw 5.20 in its tested-bodies page, fw 2.00 in four of its reverse-engineering pages), an X-T5 fw 4.20 (banks only), and an X-Pro3 fw 2.00 (banks fail). A static analysis of X RAW Studio's Windows binaries and its `XRFC.DAT` capability table. An unmerged pull request (#2, September 18, 2026) carries an X-T30 II fw 2.04 report, cited here as Community.

Relayed material: Owner reports it collected on the X-T3-era white balance shift store.

Used here: From the X-H2, the Monochromatic Color axes menu-confirmed, their 10-step grid, the D-Range Priority values and interlock, all fourteen white balance modes written and read back, `0xD186`/`0xD187` as `X-H2_0200`, `0xD184` as `FF179501,FA179501`, the bank/live split, 61 advertised properties, `GetDevicePropDesc` behavior, the seven-bank bench and its settle delay, and the apparent 25-character limit in the camera's own name entry (not an independently established USB limit; see [camera bodies](docs/07-cameras.md)).

From its static analysis: `0xD18E` as size × aspect ratio and its fallback, the Image Quality remap, the Kelvin lists and free-Kelvin bodies, half-step tones per body, the film simulation chain, the grain composite, the live-property map, the **Copy to CAMERA** dialog with its optional live write and `0xD1A5` as the live Image Size, the per-generation capability table, the `0x9001` label, and `0xD1A3` as `lLMOMode`.

Also the X-H2, X-T5, and X-Pro3 rows in the camera table, the warranty report in the README, and the X-T3-era white balance shift caution.

Its DR Auto value, conflicting live-LMO write-coverage statements, two film simulation tables, settle delay, and two static-analysis claims are in errata. The all-23 live verification report and the status page disagree; neither is treated as independently reproduced coverage here.

### [Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes](https://github.com/Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes)

License: none granted in its repository tree, README, or experiment log.

Reported measurements: From a GFX100RF through ImageCaptureCore on macOS 26 and iOS 26, the register identities explicitly marked measured, its three USB modes and their property counts, the bank selector persisting across sessions and reset by a bank change on the body, `0xD191` = 0 required for the tone writes, Clarity refused while the format is HEIF, `0xA001` during a name-length probe, Sharpness −20 read back as 65516, and the iOS-versus-macOS comparison in tether mode. The [historical Clarity report](https://github.com/Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes/blob/d453df2/README.md#L135-L136) also records success after switching to JPEG, without identifying bank versus live format or the more controlled same-session bank comparison performed on the X-T50. The name probe gives no failing payload or length, so it doesn't establish a name-length limit or isolate the cause of the refusal.

Relayed and unresolved material: The [source's provenance table](https://github.com/Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes/blob/f662d3c/README.md#L318-L327) credits gosku/Filmcase (an X-S10) for the D-Range Priority values, the NR table, and ranges. Its DR Auto `65535`, white balance list, and film simulation table (with `4`–`10` in another order) carry no per-table measurement mark; specific Filmcase lineage for DR Auto, white balance, and grain isn't established by that credit. Its white balance list contains ten modes, omitting Underwater and Custom 1–3; it documents no Kelvin trial condition. The register row historically marks toning-zero refusal and the `0xD193`/`0xD194` identities as community-derived, while the later [experiment log](https://github.com/Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes/blob/f662d3c/EXPERIMENTS.md) also asserts zero refusal among its results. No detailed transaction trace, selected simulation, or numeric response accompanies that assertion, leaving its measurement provenance unresolved. Its `0xD1B2` values are untested there.

Used here: Items explicitly marked measured at GFX100RF scope, plus attributed community, unmarked, and disputed-provenance reports; the latter do not establish additional measurements. Its macOS-versus-iOS comparison is cited with its body and mode scope. Its film simulation order, its live-settings and iOS claims, its toning-zero row, its response-code name, and its withdrawn reading of `0xD1A4` are in errata.

### [karaolidis/fujicli](https://github.com/karaolidis/fujicli)

### [gosku/Filmcase](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase)

License: GPL-3.0 (its name and branding reserved).

Reported measurements: From one X-S10 (X-Trans IV, X-Processor 4; firmware unstated), C1–C4 bank reads scored against recipes' EXIF (March 2026). The March 26 write/readback results cover film simulations `1`–`18`; fourteen white balance modes and nineteen shift values per axis; four Dynamic Range and four D-Range Priority values; five grain write codes; three values each for Color Chrome Effect and FX Blue; nine each for NR, Color, and Sharpness; seven whole-step values each for Highlight and Shadow; and eleven for Clarity. A separate Kelvin write of 5200 is reported to read back as 5200; the verifier has no Kelvin case. Toning has three reported points per axis: −180, 0, and +180. The source reports visual body checks for D-Range Priority, both toning axes, and grain Off. The historical verifier compares readbacks without retaining write response codes, so this isn't a response-confirmed acceptance matrix.

The [March 27 push fixes](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/commit/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931) report that the camera returns readbacks as uint16 (a written −5 read back as 65531; the reply width isn't recorded), Grain Off normalization, and Kelvin resetting the shifts, through a [push helper](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931/src/domain/camera/operations.py#L56-L85) that uses a [4-byte integer writer](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931/src/domain/camera/ptp_usb_device.py#L354-L362). That width comes from the contemporaneous implementation, not a capture. The commit doesn't name the body; Filmcase's documentation at that revision names only the X-S10. Current push code checks property-write responses and optionally reads successful writes back, excluding Grain Off. Issue #5 holds an X-S10 `GetDeviceInfo` (59 properties) and actual DR400 refusal/success logs, which show neither payload width nor readback. No captures published.

Relayed material: Fujifilm's camera-control SDK header for the white balance values and Color Chrome Weak; FilmKit for Nostalgic Neg. and the write-verification idea; and libfuji for the non-bank codes and the USB-mode values. fuji-ptp-notes explicitly credits Filmcase for D-Range Priority, NR, and ranges. Matching DR Auto, white balance, or grain values alone don't establish the lineage of those other tables.

Used here: DR Auto `0xFFFF` read from a bank; the four D-Range Priority values written, read back, and body-checked; NR −4, −2, and 0 from named banks, with all nine test values reported to read back as written; grain `6`/`7` read and `1` over a Large bank reading back `7`; −180, 0, and +180 toning reported to read back on both axes (not an established end-stop); C1–C4 read; the 59-property X-S10 dataset; ISO gating at base ISO 160; a Kelvin write zeroing the shifts; a Kelvin write of 5200 reported to read back as 5200; readbacks reported as uint16; and reported push/readback behavior through a 4-byte integer write path (the historical verifier uses 2 bytes). Its EXIF polarity note and its "every X-Trans IV body works" expectation are in errata.

License: GPL-3.0-or-later.

Reported measurements: From an X-E5 (its author's body), the upload-once live preview, grain `6`/`7` on factory banks, and `0xD18C` refusing 8 and above. From an X100F and an X-T3, bank writes through the settings-backup object. From an X-S20 through its issues #108 and #114, 59 advertised properties without `0xD198`, four banks, and conversions.

Used here: The upload-once live preview; the reported −2…+3 EV range and the fallback to 0 EV below it; free Kelvin on X-Processor 5; that as-shot white balance comes from a multiplier triple in the RAF and that a foreign body's raw data converts inside a native carrier RAF; bank records in the settings backup on the reported X100F and X-T3; `0x2019` from an unresponsive body; and output formats per body. ## Maintainer hardware records
