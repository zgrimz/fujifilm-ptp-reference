# Open questions

This page lists what isn't known yet, and what would settle each question. Answers from bodies other than the X-T50 are especially welcome. To send one, see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Where a published source has since answered a question, the entry says so and at what evidence level. A published hardware report remains attributed to its source; entries stay open where independent reproduction, broader body coverage, or a conflicting account is unresolved.

## Evidence and provenance

The [evidence index](../evidence/README.md) lists the published X-T50 packages and the requests still to make upstream.

Two ExifTool discussions, [topic 10800](https://exiftool.org/forum/index.php?topic=10800.0) and [topic 14319](https://exiftool.org/forum/index.php?topic=14319.0), were read only as indexed text; the tag identities, signedness, and polarity they support were checked against pinned ExifTool code instead. [Apple thread 765768](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/765768) was read in full: it is a user report of persistent denial after a reset call, not a confirmed cause. The maintainers hold the GFX100S diagnostics and September 29 JPEG pair, the X-T20 log, and the X-T50 toning captures with their output files; the peer project's proprietary-binary analysis is cited as published, not repeated.
