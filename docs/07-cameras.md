## X-T50

The reference body for this record: everything marked [Measured] was established on it.

`GetDeviceInfo` (fw 1.32): 367 bytes, PTP version 100, vendor extension `0x00000006` `fujifilm.co.jp: 1.0; `, no events advertised. [Measured: X-T50 fw 1.32] Fw 1.31 advertised the same 61 properties and 20 operations, read `0xD16E = 6` in **USB RAW CONV.** mode, `0xD184 = "FF189504,FA189504"`, and `0xD186 = 0xD187 = "X-T50_0100"`. [Published: FilmKit issue #12 log]

- Operations (20): `0x1001 0x1002 0x1003 0x1004 0x1005 0x1006 0x1007 0x1008 0x1009 0x100A 0x100B 0x1014 0x1015 0x1016 0x1017 0x900C 0x900D 0x901D 0x100C 0x100D`
- Properties (61): `0x5005 0x5015 0xD001 0xD007 0xD008 0xD00A 0xD00B 0xD00C 0xD017 0xD018 0xD01C 0xD023 0xD029 0xD02E 0xD030 0xD031 0xD032 0xD104 0xD16E 0xD17B 0xD183 0xD184 0xD185 0xD186 0xD187 0xD189 0xD18C 0xD18D 0xD18E … 0xD1A5 0xD208 0xD20B 0xD212 0xD21C 0xD320 0xD321 0xD34D 0xD36A 0xD36B`
- Capture formats: `0x3801 0x3812 0x3800 0xB103 0x380D`. Image formats add `0x5000` and `0xF802` (RAF).

### Bank behavior

- Seven banks, all selectable; every bank answers all 23 properties, and `0xD1A5`. An unnamed bank's `0xD18D` answers three bytes, a count of `1` and the NUL. [Published: FilmKit issue #12 log, fw 1.31]
- The body refuses `GetDevicePropDesc` with `0x2002` for every bank property. [Measured: fw 1.32]
- `0x500F` (ISO) isn't advertised and answers `0x200A`. [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-09-18]
- The full ISO gating and D-Range Priority interlock in [custom banks](04-custom-slots.md) were measured here.
- A bank with Sports Finder Mode on refused `0xD18E`, including a write of its current value, on C1 and later on C2; no on/off comparison on one bank isolates the mode as the cause. [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-09-06 and 2026-09-15] A separate run received `0x201C` for `D18E = 7` after turning Digital Tele-Converter on and `0x2001` after turning it off, both reading back `7`; the magnification wasn't recorded. [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-15; [sanitized run](../evidence/xt50-20261015-232441/README.md)]
- The physical Film Simulation dial overrides a bank's film simulation unless it's set to C. [Measured]
- `0xD186` and `0xD187` both read `X-T50_0100` at firmware 1.31 and 1.32. [Published: FilmKit issue #12 log, fw 1.31] [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-15]
- The camera's menu is unavailable while connected in **USB RAW CONV.** mode, so a menu sweep is a series of cable reconnects. The bank selector survives them. [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-15]
- The final controlled tests establish the red-dot behavior for the tested X-T50 USB writes: name-write timing didn't clear the dot, **RESET THE CHANGES** kept the value, and opening the flagged item or power cycling cleared the dot while retaining the value. The marker was cosmetic in those tests. For the final test results, see [persistence and the red dot](04-custom-slots.md#persistence-and-the-red-dot).
- The block addresses each bank's working copy: an Image Size changed in the shooting menu with the body on C7, left unsaved, read back as C7's value with the body on C6 too, while the camera marked C7 as modified in the Q menu and in **EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING**, and **RESET THE CHANGES** reverted it. In the later runs a USB property write took effect at once (the Q menu showed it), survived a bank change, and stayed in place after **RESET THE CHANGES**, while the camera flagged it with a red dot in **EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING**. The tested name-write timings didn't clear the flag (straight after, 10 s after, across a new PTP session, across a reconnect); a power cycle cleared it and kept the write, and so did opening the item and backing out. For details, see [the complete observations](04-custom-slots.md#persistence-and-the-red-dot). [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-16; AUTO UPDATE CUSTOM SETTING state not recorded]
- Film simulation values `4`–`7`, `10`, and `12`–`15` are menu-confirmed. `0xD1A3` is Lens Modulation Optimizer (`1` **ON**, `2` **OFF**) and `0xD1A4` Color Space (`1` sRGB, `2` Adobe RGB). A Sepia bank refused the tested toning writes; an ACROS bank refuses only Color among the color-only properties, and accepts `0` and the ±180 grid on the toning axes. The body refuses Clarity while the bank's file format is HEIF, and the gate follows the bank, not the body. The body refuses `0` on `0xD190`. The tested changes to `0xD18E` and the body's own Image Size left `0xD1A5`, X RAW Studio's live Image Size, unchanged. The tested write to `0xD1A5` produced no visible Image Size change; whether it was ignored, stored, or conditionally applied remains open. [Measured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-15]

The October 15–16 runs behind these measurements are published as [sanitized packages](../evidence/README.md#available-x-t50-packages): every transaction in source order with its scalar payload, the before/after bank snapshots, and the operator's reported menu observations. AUTO UPDATE state and a controlled Sports Finder comparison remain unrecorded.

### Conversion

`0xD185` is 625 bytes / 29 parameters / IOPCode `FF189504`. Conversion is verified end to end, including the DR behavior in [RAW conversion](05-raw-conversion.md#dynamic-range) and both Monochromatic Color axes, warm–cool in cell 23 and magenta–green in cell 26, with the renders measured on the saved output files. [Measured: fw 1.32] [Captured: fw 1.32, 2026-10-17] The FilmKit #12 capture adds a one-setting-at-a-time walk through X RAW Studio's editor at fw 1.31, which settles cells 4, 21, and 22 and gives one-variable evidence for most others. For details, see [the editor-walk transcript](../captures/x-rawstudio-editor-walk-xt50-xt30.md). A frame shot with Lens Modulation Optimizer On reads `1` in cell 21 as shot; frames shot with it Off read `2`. [Captured: fw 1.32]

## GFX100S

The first medium-format, Bayer-sensor body with any evidence, from its owner's diagnostic sessions. [Owner report: fw 2.13, 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-29]

`GetDeviceInfo`: 601 bytes in **USB RAW CONV.** mode, advertising 168 properties: `0x5001`–`0x501C` (sparse), `0xD001`–`0xD01C`, `0xD100`–`0xD161`, `0xD200`–`0xD21B`, `0xD406`, and `0xD407`. The bank block `0xD18C`–`0xD1A5` isn't advertised, but answers. In storage mode the same body gave 261 bytes and four properties; see [the wrong USB mode](01-transport.md#recognizing-the-wrong-usb-mode). It advertises no RAF image format (`0x3800 0x3801 0xB103 0x380D`, no `0xF802`) and takes RAF uploads anyway. A [libgphoto2 dump of another GFX100S at fw 1.10](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2/blob/92171a382f185f617e95cb9acecf2d43688b19ab/camlibs/ptp2/cameras/fuji-gfx-100s.txt) answered `0x200A` to a descriptor for `0xD15D`, the code libfuji and libgphoto2 name SetUSBMode. [Published: libgphoto2 camera dump, paraphrased]

### Banks

- Six banks: selecting 7 answers `0x200A`. The manual also says C1–C6.
- Every bank answers every property, including banks never programmed.
- Values decode with the same tables as the X-T50. The owner compared all six banks against the camera's own menu, 12 rows each, with no differences. That covered DR Auto `0xFFFF`, grain read values `3` and `6`, NR `0x8000` = −4, Fluorescent 2 `0x8002` with shifts R−2/B−2, and sharpness −20 = −2.
- Housekeeping values differ from the X-T50: Image Size `14`, Image Quality `2`, and `0xD1A5` `14` in every bank, against the X-T50's `7`, `4`, and `7`.
- `0xD1A5` refuses writes, even of its current value.
- The body refuses `GetDevicePropDesc` with `0x200A` for every bank property. `0xD183` gives a descriptor: UINT16, read/write, default `65535`, enumeration `[0, 1]`.
- The September 15 write pass worked: name first, settings, power cycle. Settings and name persisted, 12 rows matched the menu, and the owner reported that the photo matched. The retained 325 ms name-write transaction belongs to the subsequent restore, not that initial push.
- D-Range Priority: `0x8000` reads as **AUTO** in the menu, and Strong (`2`) writes and reads back. Turning it off before setting DR400 works. A bank at base ISO showed **OFF** in the menu while storing `0x8000`.
- The body accepted DR400 on one bank and refused it on two others, with the same write payload. This is bank-dependent refusal; the recorded snapshots do not establish ISO as the cause.
- Monochromatic Color: both axes; the write pass's bank read WC +2 / MG −1 in the camera's menu. Separate [menu photographs](../evidence/gfx100s-monochromatic-color-menu.md) establish −18…+18 menu endpoints on both axes. USB acceptance of ±180 and out-of-range rejection remain untested here. [Owner report: GFX100S fw 2.13, menu photographs, received 2026-10-18]
- No Film Simulation dial anywhere on the GFX line.

grawji's bank-enablement guide assigns the GFX100S to the settings-backup path by assumption; its runtime routes on whether `0xD18C` is advertised; the owner report in this section shows the unadvertised block answering and taking a full write pass. See [errata](errata.md#grawji).

### Conversion

`0xD185` answered `0x2002` until a RAF had been uploaded; afterward it was 621 bytes / 28 parameters / IOPCode `FF149502`, with the table at `0x1FD`. A 208,071,296-byte RAF (198.43 MiB) uploaded in 5.392 s; `GetObjectHandles` returned no handles before or after that upload, so no uploaded RAF was enumerated in that run. The supplied 5824×4368 JPEG pair closely matches the camera's own conversion statistically; the [original-file comparison](05-raw-conversion.md#proving-a-conversion-matches-the-cameras-own) confirms matching percentiles and luminance mean/standard deviation to two decimal places, with differing pixels.

## X-T20

The first X-Trans III / X-Processor Pro dataset. [Owner report: fw 2.01, received 2026-09-14; the log carries no date]

`GetDeviceInfo`: 595 bytes, same vendor extension as the X-T50.

- Operations (27): `0x1001`–`0x100B`, `0x100E 0x100F 0x1014 0x1015 0x1016 0x1017 0x1018 0x101B 0x101C 0x900C 0x900D 0x901D 0x9801 0x9802 0x9803 0x9805`. Every operation RAW conversion uses is present.
- Properties (168): `0x5001`–`0x501C` (sparse), `0xD001`–`0xD01C`, `0xD100`–`0xD161`, `0xD200`–`0xD21B`, `0xD406`, `0xD407`. Nothing from `0xD162` to `0xD1FF`.

| Property | Value read | `GetDevicePropDesc` |
|--|--|--|
| `0xD18C`–`0xD1A5` | `0x200A`, uniformly | `0x200A`, uniformly |
| `0xD183` | `0x200A` | answers: UINT16, read/write, default `65535`, enumeration `[0, 1]` |
| `0xD185` | `0x2002` | `0x200A` |

Direct bank-property access is unsupported in the reported X-T20 fw 2.01 diagnostic session: all 26 reads and descriptors for `0xD18C`–`0xD1A5` return `0x200A`. The original log records granted host authorization and successful sessions, but no USB mode or `0xD16E` result. Its **C1** report heading does not establish a successful selector write. grawji reaches an X100F's banks, and expects to reach the X-T20's, through the settings-backup object instead [Published: grawji]; that path is untested on an X-T20.

RAW conversion is officially supported: [Fujifilm's X-T20 firmware 2.00 notes](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/en-gb/support/download/firmware/cameras/x-t20/) add USB conversion with X RAW Studio, so the reported fw 2.01 is in the supported range. The open question is whether this record's third-party upload/profile/trigger/retrieval sequence works on it; the supplied log contains no upload or completed conversion. Fujifilm also lists the X-H1, X-Pro3, X-Pro2, X-T2, and X-E3 as supported. [Published: Fujifilm X RAW Studio compatibility list] The published static analysis says older bodies fail the `0xD186` read with SDK error `0x1005`, and that X RAW Studio then builds the capability key as the model name plus `_0100`; it lists no bodies. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes, static analysis]

Transport: the first `GetDeviceInfo` after a cold session took 1.56 s; later ones took 9–19 ms.

## X100VI

FilmKit's reference body. Bank reads and writes work, and conversions complete. [Published: FilmKit] [Community] A libgphoto2 camera dump of an X100VI at fw 1.00 gives a `GetDeviceInfo` of the X-T50's shape: the same 20 operations in the same order, no events, and 61 properties including the bank block, `0xD183`/`0xD185`, `0xD34D`, and `0xD36A`/`0xD36B`. `GetDevicePropDesc` answered `0x2002` for every advertised property, `0x5005` and `0x5015` included, as the X-H2 does. The **USB RAW CONV.** mode attribution is inferred from that shape; neither the dump nor the [originating report](https://github.com/gphoto/libgphoto2/issues/964) establishes the mode. [Published: libgphoto2 camera dump, paraphrased] Exposure Bias in profile cell 5 round-trips, with macnow's fork. [Community: FilmKit issue #6] FilmKit's code says the body refuses `0` on `0xD193` and offers ±18 on both toning axes; FilmKit describes no run that backs either. [Published: FilmKit]

## X-H2

The second X-Trans V body with per-property evidence, from fujifilm-ptp-recipes' bench on an X-H2. Its tested-bodies page gives the firmware as 5.20; its reverse-engineering pages describe a fw 2.00 bench. The differing labels do not establish that these were the same session. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes]

- `GetDeviceInfo` lists 61 properties, the X-T50's count. The bank block and every live code in the [live-property map](02-properties.md#live-shooting-properties) answer.
- Seven banks. A seven-bank write bench ran without error with a 50 ms pause after each select; 0 ms between property writes worked in the source's application testing. The source reports no failure without the pause.
- `GetDevicePropDesc` answers an empty data phase and then `0x2002` for every property, standard ones included.
- D-Range Priority reads and writes Off `0`, Weak `1`, Strong `2`, and Auto `0x8000`. With it on, the body refuses `0xD190`, `0xD19D`, and `0xD19E` with `0x201C`, and `0xD190` reads `0xFFFF`; with it Off, the body accepts the same values. A controlled pair of runs established this.
- All 14 white balance modes were written, read back, and restored, including Custom 1–3 (`0x8008`–`0x800A`) and `0x8020`/`0x8021`.
- Monochromatic Color: `0xD193` is warm–cool and `0xD194` magenta–green, menu-confirmed (+5 and −3 read `50` and `−30`); the body accepts only multiples of 10, in a sweep that reached `100` and `−90`; it refuses both on a color simulation; it accepted `0`.
- The bank block and the live state are separate: C7's `0xD192` read `12` while the live `0xD001` read `4`. Its live-state report says all 23 live codes read and wrote, but its protocol-status page says live LMO was read and never written; that source contradiction remains unresolved. See [the transport comparison](01-transport.md#the-advertised-lists-are-not-complete).
- The source [reports direct acceptance at both `0xD1A5` and `0xD18E`](https://github.com/ILFforever/fujifilm-ptp-recipes/blob/daba2d97d6b37bd1d5dbc68ca974f6b22f433071/docs/current-shooting-state.md#when-it-applies), with a write test changing `7` to `8`. This is a published test result for both codes, separate from the source application's normal write policy.
- The source says the camera's own name entry appears to stop at 25 characters.
- `0xD186` and `0xD187` both read `X-H2_0200`; `0xD184` reads `FF179501,FA179501`.

Conversion: no inspected source supplies a report of a successful third-party conversion on an X-H2, so that sequence is unverified here. No X-H2 profile binary was found in those sources; the published IOPCodes listed earlier are a separate observation.

## X-T5

Bank reads, property writes, and name writes work at firmware 4.20, with seven banks; fujifilm-ptp-recipes gives no per-property detail for it. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes] fujicli, whose only extensively tested body it is (firmware unstated), adds: backup and restore work; RAW conversion works end to end; its working implementation describes a 29-cell camera-read profile with IOPCode `FF179502`, without a supplied raw profile fixture; cell 1 is reported to read `2`. [Published: fujicli] A separate fujicli code comment reports that a write to `0xD1A3` is refused while no lens is attached, without identifying the body; the README's X-T5 testing scope is only indirect context. [Published: fujicli code comment] X RAW Studio's own saved profile of an X-T5 frame carries IOPCode `FA179502`. [Published: libfuji — the FP files] libgphoto2's X-Trans V additions were observed on an X-T5, including `0xD02E` as the live D-Range Priority by a differential probe. [Published: libgphoto2]

## X-Pro3

Bank reads, property writes, and name writes fail with the tested protocol path at firmware 2.00 in **USB RAW CONV./BACKUP RESTORE**, as the source's [tested-bodies table](https://github.com/ILFforever/fujifilm-ptp-recipes/blob/daba2d97d6b37bd1d5dbc68ca974f6b22f433071/docs/tested-bodies.md) explicitly records. The cause is undiagnosed and transaction response codes are not supplied. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes] grawji's [bank-enablement guide](https://github.com/p5k369/grawji/blob/7cd98fe/docs/enable-bank-writing.md#L21-L27) assigns the X-Pro3, X100V, and X-T4 to the settings-backup path by assumption, while its [feature matrix](https://github.com/p5k369/grawji/blob/7cd98fe/docs/feature-matrix.md#L90-L93) expects the property path from the X-Pro3 onward. Its runtime routes on whether `0xD18C` is advertised; the guides do not establish a settled body classification. [Published: grawji]

Fujifilm officially supports X-Pro3 RAW conversion in X RAW Studio; libfuji separately publishes its conversion profiles. [Published: Fujifilm X RAW Studio compatibility list] [Published: libfuji] X RAW Studio's [version 1.8.0 notes](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/en-us/support/download/software/x-raw-studio/) also add profile-to-camera custom-setting transfer for X-Pro3 or newer. The failed third-party bank path therefore needs diagnosis; it does not establish that official custom-setting transfer is unavailable. One `GetDeviceInfo` and `0xD18C` read would narrow that diagnosis, but demonstrating bank support requires a controlled select, per-bank reads, and write/readback results.

## X-T3 and X-T30

Direct bank editing is unavailable in the documented X-T3 fw 5.11 **USB RAW CONV.** runs: FilmKit's log reports failed selects 1–7 and only 5 of 154 vendor properties reading. [Published: FilmKit issue #5] The [independently decoded preset-change capture](../captures/x-rawstudio-bank-availability-xt3-xt30.md) confirms 168 advertised properties with the `0xD162`–`0xD1FF` hole, 27 operations, and `0xD16E = 6`. Writes to `0xD38C`, `0xD207`, `0xD001`, and `0xD040` all receive `0x200A`, while `0xD185` reads/writes and `0xD183` conversion starts receive OK. [Captured: fw 5.11] The separate connection capture contains outbound selects 1–7 but no captured response payloads, so those selection failures remain Published log evidence. What `0xD38C` means remains open. grawji writes the X-T3's banks by patching its settings backup, with a checksum the camera verifies. [Published: grawji]

No working direct bank path is established for the X-T30 fw 2.01 by the supplied evidence. FilmKit's inline log reports slot-1 failure [Published: FilmKit issue #13]; none of the three inspected captures contains a `0xD18C`–`0xD1A5` operation. The Profile_Read capture records 27 operations, zero advertised properties, and `0xD16E = 6` at tids 10 and 40, directly establishing **USB RAW CONV.** for that captured session. [Captured: FilmKit #13] Its profile read is 605 bytes, `numParams` 24, IOPCode `FF159502`, with `0xD184` reading `FF159502,FA159502`; X RAW Studio wrote 629 bytes back, filling cells 24–29 with defaults on its first write. Its preset-save capture holds only profile writes and conversions: no bank property was written in those supplied captures. Its editor walk repeats the X-T50's cell 21 and 22 results. rawji reports working tone parameters using its constructed 632-byte form with header count 29 (its indexing counts 29 parameters after a separate leading cell). That establishes a working form, not that expansion is necessary for tone edits. Its handling of the short native form also differs in offset, so the implementation does not isolate parameter count as the cause. See [alternative write forms](06-conversion-profile.md#the-host-write-form). [Published: rawji]

## GFX100RF

The second GFX body with evidence, measured through ImageCaptureCore from macOS 26 and from iOS 26 in August 2026. [Published: fuji-ptp-notes] The source marks each register as measured or community; its value tables mostly carry no mark, and it credits several to Filmcase's X-S10, so the following list separates the measured items from relayed values and notes the conflicting provenance.

- Three USB modes, three property lists: **USB CARD READER** advertises 5 properties, **USB RAW CONV.** 61 (the X-T50's and X-H2's count), and **USB TETHER SHOOTING** 286. In tether mode `0xD18C` and `0xD18D` are advertised and `0xD18E`–`0xD1A5` aren't, yet the block answered and took a write from macOS. In **USB RAW CONV.** mode the bank block is advertised and the live settings aren't, from either host; the X-T50 advertises all 20 live twins fuji-ptp-notes maps in the same mode (see [errata](errata.md#fuji-ptp-notes)).
- Seven banks.
- Measured there: the register identities explicitly marked measured; `0xD191` must be `0` for the tones to take writes; Clarity refused for every value while the format is HEIF; Sharpness `−20` read back as `65516` to an unsigned decoder; the selector behavior described later in this list; `0xA001` during a name-length probe (the failing payload and length aren't supplied); `GetDevicePropDesc` `0x2002` in **USB RAW CONV.** mode while values read fine.
- Listed without a mark, or credited to Filmcase's X-S10: DR Auto `0xFFFF`, the four D-Range Priority values, the nine NR values, the menu ranges, the white balance values, the Kelvin-only-under-Kelvin rule, and a film simulation table whose values `4`–`10` are in another order (see [errata](errata.md#fuji-ptp-notes)). The original toning rows are community-marked, while the later [experiment log](https://github.com/Junsudo/fuji-ptp-notes/blob/f662d3c/EXPERIMENTS.md) reports GFX100RF refusals of `0` on both axes, including under B&W. It supplies no detailed transaction trace, selected simulation, or numeric response code for that result, so the source's conflicting provenance and the unverified failure condition remain explicit here. No GFX body has menu-confirmed the film simulation range here.
- `0xD18C` keeps its last written value across a closed session and a cable reconnect, and reports that rather than the bank the body is on; until a host writes it, and again after a bank change on the camera, it reads the body's bank.
- The source lists `0xD1A5 = 7` without a measured basis or comparison to `0xD18E`. The source identifies `0xD1B2` as a live image-format selector; its values and write behavior are untested there.

Conversion: officially supported by [X RAW Studio](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/global/support/compatibility/software/x-raw-studio/). [Published: Fujifilm X RAW Studio compatibility list] The third-party upload/profile/trigger/retrieval sequence documented here has not been reproduced on a GFX100RF. The published static capability table additionally lists HEIF output. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes, static analysis]

## X-S10

Filmcase reports an X-S10 (X-Trans IV / X-Processor 4), without a firmware version in the inspected material. Its evidence includes bank reads scored against recipes' EXIF and [March 26, 2026 write/readback results](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/a272979c851d1f1bd4db36572d3f117a697e3720/docs/ptp_encodings.md#L14-L34). The historical verifier discarded write response codes before reselecting the bank and comparing readbacks; the current push path's response checks don't supply those missing historical responses. No captures are published. [Published: X-S10, Filmcase]

- `GetDeviceInfo` in **USB RAW CONV.** mode: [59 advertised properties](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/issues/5), with `0xD198` omitted from `0xD18C`–`0xD1A5` (the X-S20's count and gap); no direct X-S10 read or write of `0xD198` is recorded. The list includes `0x5005` and `0x5015`; its only missing member of the record's 23-code live mirror is `0xD189` (Smooth Skin). `0xD1B2` is also unadvertised, but isn't a member of that mirror. `0xD16E` reads `6`. The cited material does not establish the corresponding bank-property behavior in **USB CARD READER** or **USB TETHER SHOOTING**; the earlier refusal comparison could not be traced to an original report.
- Banks: C1–C4 read with differing values; no select of 5 is reported. The write/readback results cover film simulations `1`–`18`; all fourteen listed white balance modes; nineteen shift values per axis (−9…+9); four Dynamic Range values, including Auto `0xFFFF`; D-Range Priority `0`/`1`/`2`/`0x8000`, also body-checked; five grain write codes; three values each for Color Chrome Effect and FX Blue; nine each for NR, Color, and Sharpness; seven whole-step values each for Highlight and Shadow; and eleven Clarity values. Grain `6`/`7` were read from banks, and `1` written over Strong + Large read back as `7`. Toning readbacks and body checks cover `−180`, `0`, and `180` on both axes, with `190` untried; this doesn't establish the full grid or maximum. Writes of `19` and `20` leave `0xD192` unchanged (the response code wasn't kept). A separate Kelvin write of `5200` is reported to read back as `5200`; the verifier has no Kelvin case, so how that test ran isn't recorded. A Kelvin write sent after the shifts zeroed them. Color writes leave `0xD19F` unchanged under a monochrome simulation. At rest C1 held `0xD18E` = `0xD1A5` = `7`, `0xD1A3` = `0xD1A4` = `1`, and toning `180`/`180` on a Classic Chrome bank.
- ISO gating: base ISO 160; [issue #5](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/issues/5) logs DR400 refused `0x201C` at ISO 250 and WRITE OK at ISO 800; at base ISO `0xD191` and DR400 both refused. The logs show neither payload width nor readback. `0xD02A` and `0x500F` answer `0x200A`.
- Firmware: the [implementation attempts `0xD153`](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/b8edef94e3c3fa80d96331d472388aabc84ec6ca/src/domain/camera/queries.py#L142-L153), catches a general connection error, and comments that the property isn't supported on the X-S10. No specific failure trace was found in the inspected material. Its [DeviceInfo parser extracts the model](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/b8edef94e3c3fa80d96331d472388aabc84ec6ca/src/domain/camera/ptp_usb_device.py#L159-L203), not DeviceVersion; that doesn't establish whether DeviceInfo could supply the firmware.
- Transport: Filmcase reports bank-push readback behavior through a 4-byte integer write path. Its [March 27 push fixes](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/commit/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931) state that the camera returns readbacks as uint16: a written −5 read back as 65531, which failed the 32-bit comparison until the fix compared only the low 16 bits. The reply width isn't recorded. The same fixes account for Grain Off normalization and Kelvin resetting the shifts. That revision's [push helper](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931/src/domain/camera/operations.py#L56-L85) calls a [4-byte integer setter](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/5652d21a45a244fc18f35e8441fe83b1be6a8931/src/domain/camera/ptp_usb_device.py#L354-L362). The width comes from the contemporaneous implementation, not a capture or a complete acceptance matrix; the historical sweep used 2-byte writes. Issue #5's bank-investigation scan records `0xD183` answering `0x200A` and `0xD185` `0x2002`; no upload is reported in that workflow. In the [browser/WebUSB path](https://github.com/gosku/Filmcase/blob/b8edef94e3c3fa80d96331d472388aabc84ec6ca/docs/ADRs/016-client-side-camera-transport.md#L78-L100), a timed-out read remains queued; a plain device reopen leads to `OpenSession` returning `0x201E`, and a USB still-image class Device Reset clears the state. The server/PyUSB path can cancel the read through libusb and retry in place.

This adds detailed Published X-S10 bank observations to FilmKit's X100V reports and the X-T30 II community report. The X-Pro3's failure still shows that the shared X-Processor 4 generation doesn't establish bank-block availability.

A manual describes the menu: which items a body has, which values each offers, and the ISO floors it states. It doesn't say what a body answers or accepts over USB, so these facts are tagged `[Published: Fujifilm manuals]` or `[Published: Fujifilm firmware notes]`, and USB evidence on the same claim outranks them.

A manual fact is recorded only where it has a USB anchor. Either it explains an observation the record holds: the ISO floors behind a refused DR write, the item that drives **TONE CURVE** behind the D-Range Priority interlock, **AUTO UPDATE CUSTOM SETTING** behind a persistence result, or HEIF forcing sRGB behind a predicted Color Space refusal. A body without protocol observations gets no row of its own and is named only where a protocol statement needs the list it belongs to, such as the strength-only grain bodies or the single-axis toning bodies. Menu facts with neither anchor stay out; per-body capability for every body Fujifilm sells belongs in a client's registry, not here.

The bodies covered are those with protocol observations in the [summary](#summary). The X100F manual is published only as a PDF and hasn't been read, so it's absent from the tables. Each table or paragraph links the page read; [`SOURCES.md`](../SOURCES.md) gives the reading dates. ## Bodies with a physical Film Simulation dial

[X-T50](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t50/about_this_camera/parts/), [X-M5](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-m5/about_this_camera/parts/), [X-E5](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-e5/about_this_camera/parts/), and [X-T30 III](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t30-iii/about_this_camera/parts/) have the dial, per their manuals' parts pages. On these the dial overrides a bank's film simulation unless it's set to C. FS1–FS3 on the X-T50 and X-M5 store film simulation/filter assignments. The X-E5 and X-T30 III additionally offer **FS RECIPE**, which, when **ON**, can store image-quality settings for those positions separately from the custom banks. See [custom banks](04-custom-slots.md#bodies-with-a-film-simulation-dial).

## Processor generations

Processor families help explain some capability patterns, but model and firmware exceptions matter: Smooth Skin, half-step tones, and the toning axes don't follow one universal generation boundary. [Value encodings](03-encodings.md) separates X-T50 measurements, GFX100S owner observations, X-H2 tests, the reported X-S10 readbacks, and broader X-T5, X-S20, and X-T30 II reports. These establish agreement for particular properties and tested values, not every table entry on each body or generation. X RAW Studio's capability tables describe its offered features, not a body's measured response. Older generations rest on published sources.

Whether the tested direct bank path works isn't predicted by the processor: the X-Pro3 path fails where the X100V, X-S10, X-T30 II, and GFX100S, on the same X-Processor 4, have successful reports. The X-Pro3 failure's cause remains undiagnosed. X RAW Studio's own capability table, decoded by fujifilm-ptp-recipes, assigns tone steps, effects, and film simulation sets by model and firmware. Half-step tones include the X-T4, X-S10, X-E4, X-T30 II, GFX100S, GFX 50S II, and the later GFX100 capability record, as well as every listed X-Processor 5 body; see [the tone variants](03-encodings.md#0xd19d--0xd19e--0xd19f--0xd1a0--0xd1a2-tones-color-sharpness-clarity). Custom white balance 1–3 is in the older WB set. Which bodies refuse the `0xD18E` composite and get its fallback is the source's guess, and that guess includes X-Trans V bodies. None of that has been checked on hardware. [Published: fujifilm-ptp-recipes, static analysis]

The magenta–green toning axis doesn't follow the processor either: Fujifilm's manuals give it to the X-Processor 4 bodies from the X-Pro3 on and to the GFX100 from firmware 4.00, and only the X-T3, the X-T30, and the GFX100 before firmware 4.00 have the warm–cool axis alone. [Published: Fujifilm manuals] See [Monochromatic Color](03-encodings.md#0xd193--0xd194-monochromatic-color).

One reported behavior of the X-T3, X-T30, and X-H1, and by the source's account of the X-Trans III bodies generally, matters to any bank writer that reaches them: the white balance shift is said to be stored once per white balance mode, not per bank, so a shift written to one bank changes every bank using that mode and still reads back as written. [Community: owner reports collected by fujifilm-ptp-recipes; not measured over USB] An X-T30 II is reported to store it per bank. [Community: fujifilm-ptp-recipes PR #2] None of the older bodies has shown the bank block over USB here.
